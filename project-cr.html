Codeway Runner

📅 2025 ⏱ 2 weeks 👥 Pair project
Javascript Android Mobile

What is it?

A partnered assignment to make an Android scroller game inspired by Subway Surfers with a unique modern twist — a coding-themed world where players dodge bugs and collect syntax tokens.

My role

I handled the player physics, obstacle spawning system, and overall game loop. My partner focused on UI screens and the achievement system.

Design process

Early concept sketch

First playable build

We started with rough sketches of the lane-switching mechanic before moving into Android Studio. The biggest early decision was whether to use Unity or build natively — we chose Android Studio to meet the assignment brief and get hands-on with Java/Kotlin.

Final game

Biggest challenge

Syncing obstacle spawn timing with the player's increasing speed was tricky — early playtests felt either too slow or impossible. We solved it by tying spawn rate to a difficulty curve variable rather than hardcoding values.

What I'd do differently

I'd plan the achievement system earlier. It was added late and required us to refactor the scoring system, which cost us time we could have spent on polish.

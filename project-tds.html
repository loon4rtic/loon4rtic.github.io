01

Overview

Total Defence Simulator is a 2D tower defence game inspired by Singapore, built in Unity by a team of four over two weeks as part of my school's work-integrated unit.

The brief

Make a 2D game inspired by Singapore's National Day.

02

Key features

Tower builder

Civil, Psychological, Military and Digital towers, named after pillars of Singapore's Total Defence.

Power-ups

Freeze and Tower Boost abilities to turn the tide when a wave gets overwhelming.

Singapore levels

Unlockable maps based on real neighbourhoods: Khatib, Bukit Batok, Clementi and Woodlands.

03

My role

I was a gameplay programmer on a team of four, working on both the core systems and the content that runs on them.

Systems
  • Tower placement system

  • Dialogue system

Gameplay & content
  • Player
  • Level design
  • Assets and animations
  • Enemy tooltips
04

Biggest challenge

Problem

We wanted the whole game to run in a single playable scene instead of a separate Unity scene per level, to save space and keep it optimised. That meant levels had to be swapped in and out while the game was running.

Solution

I programmed a level loader that reads each level from an Excel file, so levels are loaded and changed within the same scene without ever switching scenes.

Reflection

What I learned

I learned how much regular meetings and check-ins smooth out development. We checked in every other day across the two weeks, so everyone stayed caught up, knew what to do next, and could see where they could help each other.

About Me

Get to know me!

I’m Shayn, a game developer and designer based in Singapore. I am currently studying for my Diploma in Game Development & Technology at Nanyang Polytechnic, where I built everything from a multiplayer co-op game to an educational VR experience for children with special needs.

I’m drawn to games that make you feel something — the dread of a horror game, the quiet comfort of a cozy indie. That tension between emotion and interaction is what drives how I think about design: every mechanic, every scene should serve the experience.

Outside of game projects, I also build for the web, which is how this portfolio came to life. I’m always looking for new problems to solve and new worlds to build — take a look at my projects to see what I’ve been working on.
